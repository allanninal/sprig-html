How we glaze the aubergine

The Miso-Glazed Aubergine is cooked whole, so the flesh stays soft and the skin chars. We brush it with a glaze of white miso and roast it until it is dark at the edges and almost creamy in the middle.

It is plated with black rice, pickled cucumber, shiso and crispy shallots, and finished with sesame. The salty, savoury miso is there to carry the sweetness of the aubergine, so we keep the other flavours bright.

Because it is made with miso and sesame, it contains soy and sesame. Whatever you eat with us, please tell your server about any allergy before you order, and we will talk it through with the kitchen.
