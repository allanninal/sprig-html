Why the blackboard changes every week

Every week at Verdant Table starts at the blackboard, not at a printed menu. We buy from eleven farms within 120 miles of Portland, and what they have picked decides what goes up on it.

Fenwick Hollow Farm sends root vegetables and brassicas, Thistledown Grain sends heritage grain and legumes, and Kestrel Bend Orchard brings stone fruit and berries when they are in season. Chef Maya Okafor visits each farm every season to choose in person.

A dish can leave the board when its vegetable does, and that is on purpose. We also run a CSA share with four local farms, which guarantees them a fair price before the season starts. Ask your server what is new this week.
